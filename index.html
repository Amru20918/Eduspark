// about eduspark

A practical technology training center in Ranjangaon, Pune

Eduspark International Pvt. Ltd. is a technology coaching and training center based in Ranjangaon, Pune, Maharashtra. We work with students, fresh graduates, and career-focused learners who want to build industry-relevant technical skills through hands-on practice rather than theory alone.

Learning at Eduspark is organized around real project work and mentor guidance, so that what you study connects directly to what you build. Each of our 7 branches is designed to take a learner from fundamentals to a working, practical understanding of the domain.
